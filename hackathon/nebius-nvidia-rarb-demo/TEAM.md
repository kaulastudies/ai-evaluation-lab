## Proposed research review responsibilities

We propose Prof. Nithya Ravi Kumar as the mentor for methodology and evidence review. The [review pack](submission/MENTOR_REVIEW_PACK.md) is prepared for her, with four substantial areas of work:

1. **Experiment design review:** assess task coverage, fixed-trial comparisons, confounding factors and the next study's scope.
2. **Verifier qualification review:** examine fault coverage, independent checks and a proposed held-out challenge set.
3. **Claims and reproducibility review:** check that each reported result has a traceable artifact and that limits are stated accurately.
4. **Demonstration review:** evaluate whether an engineer can follow the failure, repair and verification sequence without confusing replay with new inference.

These responsibilities are proposed, not a record of completed reviews. The pack remains awaiting her review. Her feedback, revisions and decisions will be attributed when they are provided. The existing code, experiments and results are not represented as authored, supervised or approved by her retroactively.
