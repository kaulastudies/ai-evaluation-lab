What does the private demonstrator cover?

Bounded startup onboarding and lifecycle, department-routed requests, sample operational registers, original lessons and assessment, event registration, scoped exports and retained action receipts. Review the requirements comparison and live QA record for the implemented scope and remaining production gates.

What still requires verification or external approval?

The direct .tech portal includes 17 roles, departmental modules, course authoring, student outreach and English/Telugu. Android and unsigned iOS simulator builds pass. Approved organisational workflows, institutional identity controls, physical-device QA, live provider integrations, India hosting, capacity/accessibility review, UAT and independent security audit remain release gates. Bidder documents, business history, client references and insurance remain separate eligibility matters.

Does viewing this page grant rights to the software?

No source-code licence or transfer is offered by this preview. Original project-specific implementation remains private and all rights are reserved. Any evaluation access, licence or commercial handover would require a separate agreement. A public interface or idea can still be observed and reproduced.
