# Qatar operating plan

Proposal dated 30 October 2026. AI Evaluation Lab seeks to validate coding-agent release checks with software teams and integrators in Qatar, then build local evaluation delivery if the pilot supports continued investment. No local customer, channel agreement, entity or relocation is committed.

## Why Qatar

AI/ML and B2B SaaS are published Startup Qatar priority sectors. Qatar's ICT supplier directory provides an identifiable starting pool for discovery, rather than a claim of qualified demand. The [buyer map](QATAR_BUYER_MAP.md) identifies research targets and the evidence required to qualify each. A local engineering release owner is the first buyer. Customer introductions, procurement context and establishment guidance are the concrete program support requested.

Potential local value is evaluation-engineering capability: reusable checks, a documented reviewer process and, after supported demand, a local technical hire. Arabic-English support evaluation is an adjacent hypothesis; it is not necessary to the first code-verification pilot.

## Proposed sequence

| Period after funded start | Delivery | Decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1-30 | Qualify named research targets; seek 15 buyer interviews; select one recurring release decision | Three teams describe the same problem; one task/release owner and budget route |
| Days 31-60 | Develop reviewed isolated staging runner; attempt three assisted installs | Authorized artifacts, setup time, baseline results, data boundary and independent review |
| Days 61-90 | Freeze pilot acceptance; seek one signed paid scope or budget-authorized LOI | Procurement path, verified scope, costed local establishment route |
| Months 4-6 | Complete first paid pilot; seek repeat release use; form locally on agreed terms | Customer acceptance, collected cash, feasible operating obligations |
| Months 7-12 | Conditional local engineer and repeat installation | Demand supports cost; funded cash covers obligations; reusable contract evidence |

The plan assumes founder time for budgeting. It does not state a full-time or residence commitment. The founder's actual answers toF6S 18,20 and 21 control. Before expenditure, confirm that the program accepts the proposed sequencing and initial allocation. No pre-incorporation, offshore-spending or remote-only entitlement is assumed.

## Commercial and data boundaries

The first pilot uses an authorized non-production repository or sanitized staging artifacts. The buyer retains authority over merge and release. No production credentials, autonomous write access or regulated personal data are needed for the proposed initial scope. Agree evidence ownership, retention, deletion, confidentiality and permitted publication before access. Security review remains a prerequisite for customer execution.

## Establishment and hiring budget

The plan includes$7,500 setup/legal/travel, $7,200 workspace/administration frommonth 4 and$21,000 engineer allowance frommonth 7. These are planning allowances, not Qatar quotations. Obtain itemized formation, visa, insurance, payroll and tax requirements before signing. Record any benefits against the exact covered cost; do not count a waiver as cash or assume all establishment costs disappear.

## Separate decisions

Startup Qatar investment and entrepreneur residency are distinct programs. Do not import an unrelated residency bank-balance requirement into the investment application as a stated condition. Choose a residence/visa route only after eligibility and actual founder plans are known. The application can disclose unresolved terms without inventing agreement.
