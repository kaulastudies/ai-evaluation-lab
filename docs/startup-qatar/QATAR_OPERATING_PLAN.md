# Conditional Qatar operating plan

Status: proposal for diligence, 30 October 2026. No Qatar entity, relocation, local hire, investment, customer or programme acceptance is asserted. The decision is whether Qatar can support a repeatable customer-funded release-verification business, with a credible local contribution.

## Why this could fit Qatar

START supports entrepreneurs with a PoC/MVP establishing Qatar operations and lists AI/ML, B2B SaaS and cybersecurity among its priority sectors.[1] AI Evaluation Lab's existing verifier and evidence tooling supports an eligibility discussion. Eligibility does not establish market demand. The commercial case must come from a local buyer who has tool-using agents, an accountable release owner, accessible workflow evidence and budget to test the service.

The initial local contribution proposed is practical: help one Qatar-based SaaS/software team or coding-agent integrator inspect whether an AI-generated change satisfies a bounded repository task contract, then transfer a maintainable regression pack to its engineers. ProofGate is the current coding-agent demonstrator. The offline support/approval example is a separate expansion hypothesis, not the first customer integration. Arabic-English cases would add value only after a qualified bilingual reviewer and a buyer validate the need. Arabic capability is not yet established. Open synthetic methods can support local technical learning while private buyer data remains in its own authorised workspace.

## First 90 days after an approved, funded start

Dates are relative to an approved, funded start, not a promised programme decision date. See the [financial plan](FINANCIAL_PLAN.md) for unchanged cost assumptions and proposed release gates. The [commercial plan](COMMERCIAL_PLAN.md), redesigned attachments and this operating plan share the same initial targets: 15 buyer interviews, three assisted staging installs and one signed paid scope or budget-authorized LOI in the first 90 days. The base financial model records first completed paid delivery and invoicing in month 4 and recurring service from month 6. An install or LOI does not equal delivery, invoicing or collected cash.

Measure installation effort and compare buyer-authored cases with normal tests; identify budget and procurement owners. | Interviews show existing tools solve the problem adequately, no budget owner appears, or access/integration cost dominates potential value. |

These are proposed roles, not a claim of a staffed department or an appointed external adviser. External review must be secured and its independence documented. Buyer interviews, LOIs and pilots cannot be completed by writing a plan; their evidence remains an open execution dependency.

## Months 4–12, conditional on gates

Form the appropriate entity and establish the permitted contracting, banking and accounting route only after written selection terms and cost confirmation make it viable. Conduct business development through consented introductions and qualified local prospects. A directory entry or public organisation name is not a partnership.

Add one local engineering role only when a paid continuation or second paying customer supports recurring work, an employment route and all-in cost are confirmed, and sufficient cash is received. The model uses a month-7 hiring assumption to test capacity; that date and the $3,500 monthly allowance are not commitments. Define the role around customer-owned regression packs, evidence connectors, reviewability and bilingual validation support where a qualified reviewer is available.

Recurring service begins in month 6 only in the illustrative base scenario, after an accepted pilot and an actual agreement. At month 6, review delivery hours, collected cash, customer renewal intent, incidents and reviewer independence. At month 12, expansion requires repeat purchasing and a supportable cost structure. A larger team, office or broad regional launch should follow that evidence.

## Residency is a separate feasibility check

The published entrepreneur-residency route requires local-incubator endorsement. For overseas applicants it also lists an attested three-month bank statement with a minimum USD 10,000 balance, passport validity, photograph, CV and attested police clearance.[2] Those are requirements of that residency route. They are not presented on the investment page as a universal START investment-application balance requirement. Ask which visa route applies to a funded founder and whether its terms differ; do not assume an investment waiver removes immigration requirements.

Keep identity documents and bank evidence private. Do not commission attestations, travel, accommodation or formation solely on the assumption of later approval. Founder relocation and minimum cash availability are unresolved decisions.

## Questions to resolve before a submission commitment

1. Can an unincorporated overseas PoC/MVP founder apply, and at what stage must the Qatar entity exist?
2. Which entity/licence route applies to evaluation software and fixed-scope engineering services, and exactly which initial and recurring costs are waived?
3. What investment instrument, ownership terms, founder presence and time commitment would be required? Is the proposed staged validation scope eligible?
4. When would funds arrive; what spending can precede disbursement; is funding a reimbursement, an advance or another structure?
5. Which visa route applies, what cash or proof-of-funds is required, and what relocation/housing costs remain?
6. What local hiring requirements and costs apply, and may the first period focus on a bounded pilot before a full-time hire?

These questions are prepared for the founder's review; no enquiry or application has been sent by this work.
