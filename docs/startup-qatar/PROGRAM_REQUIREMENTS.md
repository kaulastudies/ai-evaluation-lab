Checked 30 October 2026 against the live public [F6S application](https://www.f6s.com/sqinvestmentprogram) and [QDB programme page and FAQ](https://www.qdb.qa/en/Financing-And-Funding/Equity-And-Investment/Startup-Qatar-Investment-Program). A later same-day fetch returned HTTP 403; the map uses the earlier readable snapshot and does not imply continuous portal access. Earlier references to “21 questions” described only the first group.

The numbering below matches the current form; short labels are paraphrases, not a verbatim form export. This public read does not establish every dropdown option, character limit, conditional validation rule or signed-in draft state. The live portal controls those details. Keep private answers and supporting records outside this repository.

## Four different kinds of requirement

| Category | Authority and meaning | Application to this proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form fields | The numbered F6S questions, uploads and declarations | Answer the actual questions accurately, including any applicable conditional fields. Exporting attachments does not complete the form. |
| Published programme criteria | QDB's START description and eligibility FAQ | Show a validated need/concept, execution plan, detailed technology roadmap, market research, viable monetization and capable committed leadership. The committee judges their sufficiency. |
| Internal jury-strengthening evidence | The Lab's proposed validation and review gates | Buyer interviews, held-out evaluation, installation evidence and a paid scope improve the case. The specific 15/3/1 targets are not published admission rules. |
| Later investment/operating diligence | Terms and obligations still to be clarified with the programme and qualified advisers | Entity timing, investment instrument, costs, cash release, residency, employment and data obligations must be resolved before the relevant commitment. Do not invent their answers. |

START is a PoC/MVP route for establishing Qatar operations. QDB advertises up to US$1.1 million, released against agreed milestones, and includes AI/ML and B2B SaaS among its priority sectors. These facts support exploring the route; they are not an award or a guarantee of eligibility. GROW's three-year operating-history criterion should not be imported into START. [QDB](https://www.qdb.qa/en/Financing-And-Funding/Equity-And-Investment/Startup-Qatar-Investment-Program) and [Startup Qatar](https://startupqatar.qa/en/investment-program).

## Evidence to strengthen the jury decision

The published criteria require substantive commercial and execution credibility, but the pages do not prescribe the Lab's specific thresholds below.

| Proposed evidence | Why it changes a review | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 buyer interviews; three teams describing the same coding-release problem | Tests the proposed need against independent buyer accounts | Target only; no completed interviews claimed |
| Three assisted staging installs with failed attempts and effort retained | Tests whether the existing demonstration can become a usable customer workflow | Target only; reviewed customer execution boundary is unbuilt |
| One signed paid scope or budget-authorized LOI by day 90 | Establishes a concrete budget owner and next decision | Target only; an LOI is not payment or delivered value |
| At least 30 buyer/reviewer-authored coding holdouts, independent review and a fair normal-process comparison | Tests verifier usefulness beyond self-authored controls | Proposed; agent-assisted review does not qualify as independent human validation |
| Month-4 paid delivery and month-6 recurring service | Tests price acceptance, cost and reuse | Timing assumptions in the base model, not bookings or commitments |
| Reconciled financials, practical capacity and a costed Qatar route | Makes the execution plan credible | Founder records, terms and local quotes remain open |

## Rule gaps to resolve without inventing requirements

The consulted pages do not settle these questions for this applicant:

- Whether and when an overseas unincorporated founder must form the Qatar entity, and which licence/activity route applies.
- Exact investment instrument, dilution, valuation, rights, allowed spend, milestone agreement, advance/reimbursement mechanics and decision/disbursement timing.
- Required founder presence, local employment timing or headcount, and how conditional answers to fields 18–21 affect selection. Questions on the form do not by themselves disclose acceptance rules.
- Exact fee-waiver scope/duration and remaining formation, visa, payroll, insurance, office, travel or other costs.
- Which visa route applies. Conditions from a separate entrepreneur-residency programme must not be labeled a universal START investment requirement.
- Accepted attachment extensions, all field limits, conditional validations, and the actual signed-in draft status/deadline. The public fetch establishes labels and the 30 MB limits, not every portal behavior.
- No published universal requirement for a paid pilot, minimum revenue, a particular customer count, our proposed case counts or performance thresholds, an independent security certification or a specified TAM number was established by this review. This does not waive QDB's broader validation and market-readiness criteria.

Unresolved facts must be disclosed accurately in the final application; unresolved commercial risks can be explained with bounded validation plans. Legal, financial and personal commitments require actual decisions and terms before execution. This requirements map does not accept terms, submit the form or authorize spending.
