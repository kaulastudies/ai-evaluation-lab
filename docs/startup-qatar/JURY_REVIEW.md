# Selection committee assessment

Assessment date:30 October 2026. This is an internal investment argument, not a prediction of the program's decision or a third-party endorsement.

## Why this merits technical diligence

AI Evaluation Lab has already made a useful failure mode inspectable: an agent can pass the available tests and still violate a task requirement. ProofGate presents retained evidence, including failed and unresolved attempts. The founder has shipped public evaluation code and an externally merged Backstage contribution. The proposed initial$12,000 allocation buys a bounded test of whether that engineering can support a paid release decision.

The question for the committee is specific: can one software team repeatedly use these checks, trust the evidence and justify the delivery cost? The proposal names the buyer, the baseline, a small blind test, an independent review role and a funding stop decision. Sourced market reference counts replace unsupported global AI-market headlines.

## Reasons to decline today

- Demand is not demonstrated by the synthetic ledger. No paying Lab customer is established by the reviewed evidence.
- Existing tests, LangSmith and Promptfoo can already implement checks. The proposed advantage is reduced contract-design/review effort and useful incremental findings, which needs measurement.
- The current offer is labor-intensive. A 25% pilot contribution can disappear after acquisition and administration. Repeatability must reduce delivery effort.
- Qatar operating commitments and historical accounts remain unresolved. A conditional plan cannot serve as a factual yes/no declaration.
- Production isolation and authenticated evidence collection remain development work. Security remediationPR #34 is separate and unmerged.

## Scale hypothesis

The reusable product would consist of versioned task contracts, negative controls and evidence review that fit into a team's release workflow. New sectors require new domain rules and authorized evidence. The code verification offer earns the right to expand. No multi-billion-dollar valuation, universal verifier or usage surge is claimed.

## Recommended committee action

Consider a technical-diligence conversation and a small conditional validation allocation if the founder's eligibility and operating commitments fit the program. Do not treat current evidence as sufficient for an unconditional expansion investment. Later funding should be the revised cost of the next accepted milestone after deducting available cash and confirmed collections.
