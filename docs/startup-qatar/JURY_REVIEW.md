# Jury review: AI Evaluation Lab / ProofGate

Assessment date: 30 October 2026. This is a simulated investment-committee assessment, not Startup Qatar's official rubric, an external review, or a prediction of selection. No verified evidence supports a claim about competing against “lakhs” of applications. Competition should be addressed through stronger proof rather than invented acceptance odds.

## Decision

**Advance to technical diligence; defer an investment recommendation.**

The reason to take a meeting is concrete: this founder has built an inspectable way to distinguish an agent's success claim from a verified result. The public repository preserves failures, controls, source information and replay instructions. That makes the central claim more credible than a pitch supported only by a polished interface.

The reason to defer investment is equally concrete: the evidence demonstrates engineering ability on bounded synthetic tasks, while the commercial case still needs a buyer, a budget, a repeatable delivery model and a viable Qatar operating arrangement. A stronger document can expose and organize those gaps. It cannot close them by describing planned pilots as completed work.

## What would earn my vote

I would advocate selection when the company can show the following chain with inspectable records:

1. A reachable buyer owns a release decision that existing checks handle poorly.
2. A frozen, independently challenged evaluation finds material failures or reduces review effort on that buyer's workflow without an unacceptable false-alarm burden.
3. The buyer accepts the evidence and agrees to a priced next engagement; delivery cost supports the proposed margin.
4. The founder has a credible plan to establish Qatar operations, with funding terms, cash needs, local execution capacity and personal commitments resolved.
5. The requested investment purchases specific evidence and milestones, with a stop or rescope decision when they fail.

A paid pilot is the strongest practical next signal here, not a published formal START requirement. If procurement delays payment, a signed, dated pilot scope with an identified budget owner and procurement path is useful but weaker evidence. An unsigned expression of interest is not a sale.

## Why the existing proof matters—and its limits

The committed RARB metrics identify **3 verifier failures among 46 attempts with explicit success claims and passing public tests**. This is evidence of a useful failure pattern in the published set; it is not an estimate of the false-completion rate in enterprises. There are **52 total attempts**, including the **30 AP-001 Phase 12 trials**. Adding those numbers would double-count evidence. The Phase 12 batch has 29 passes, one failure, no false greens and no repairs; do not attribute the aggregate's three false greens to that batch.

The verifier qualification records report **zero escapes among 17 configured critical mutations**. This establishes performance on those controls, not complete fault coverage. The new workflow example is original synthetic demonstration material. It is not an additional customer, live-model experiment, deployment or externally validated benchmark.

The parent project is **AI Evaluation Lab**; **ProofGate** is an existing application on the Lab's repository-agent work. Other research prototypes should remain supporting work rather than additional startup product lines in this application. A focused buyer story is easier to evaluate than a portfolio of unrelated future markets.

## Questions I would ask in the final room

**Why should I fund you when established evaluation platforms already exist?**

The testable proposal is a specialist implementation and evidence package for a costly workflow release decision. Existing platforms already support custom checks, regression evaluation and human review. Our case must demonstrate that the qualified contracts, evidence-of-effect checks and reproducible adjudication improve the buyer's decision at an acceptable total cost. We have not established a unique feature monopoly or a defensible moat yet.

**What exactly have you proved?**

A working evaluation and replay pipeline, five synthetic repository tasks, a retained 52-attempt evidence set, and detection of specified false greens and control mutations. We have not proved general agent safety, enterprise ROI or production reliability. The [evidence ledger](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) defines the denominators and provenance.

**Who is the first customer?**

No paying AI Evaluation Lab customer is verified in the public evidence used for this review. The first proposed customer profile is a team deploying an agent into a bounded support/approval workflow, with an engineering owner and access to a budget decision. Interviews and a paid pilot must establish whether that profile is real and reachable.

**Why Qatar?**

The application proposes local validation, delivery capacity and eventually qualified Arabic-English review for relevant customers. Sector alignment alone is insufficient. We need direct local buyer evidence and an operating plan whose commitments the founder can honor. Introductions, research contacts and published ecosystem initiatives are not partnerships.

**Why this amount of money?**

Use the costed milestone plan, not the program's maximum. Show how each tranche reduces a specific technical, commercial or operational uncertainty. The new planning model is separate from the existing public $10,000 grant request; overlapping costs must not be financed twice.

**What happens if the pilot finds no incremental value?**

Retain the result. Stop expansion, determine whether the contract, workflow or product hypothesis failed, and either rescope once with buyer evidence or discontinue that wedge. A null result cannot be converted into a success metric by changing the acceptance criteria afterward.

**Can a malicious or faulty system fabricate its own evidence?**

Hashes establish consistency of retained artifacts, not that an external event happened. A customer deployment needs a trustworthy receipt source, access controls and a separate verification boundary. Missing or conflicting evidence should prevent an automatic success conclusion. The offline demo does not establish that deployment trust boundary.

**Can you execute with the present team?**

The repository demonstrates founder execution. It does not demonstrate a staffed commercial organization. Scope the first engagement to available capacity, obtain independent review, and hire against funded, measured demand. Mentorship is acknowledged; completed review or institutional endorsement must not be inferred.

## Evidence that matters most next

Priority one is a buyer-owned pilot and measured baseline comparison. Priority two is independent verifier and security review. Priority three is a quote-backed Qatar operating plan with founder commitments and investment terms resolved. These are more likely to change an investment decision than more generic features, a larger TAM slide or additional unvalidated product names.

The committee should receive a concise deck and a reproducible evidence bundle with explicit unknowns. The source [application draft](APPLICATION_DRAFT.md) is intentionally not ready to submit while its OPEN items remain unresolved.
