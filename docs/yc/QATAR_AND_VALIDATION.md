## A bounded pilot route

The Qatar buyer map prioritizes iHorizons, malomatia, Mannai and Advancya as research targets based on their published engineering and integration work. None is represented as a customer, partner, introduction or willing buyer. Begin by identifying a release owner, the last relevant failure and who controls a pilot budget.

Use a synthetic or authorized staging repository. Freeze the acceptance rules before scoring the agent's output. Compare the existing tests with the proposed checks and have the buyer review disputed outcomes. Record installation hours, defects additionally caught, false rejections, reviewer time, execution cost and ongoing maintenance. A demonstrated failure that is cheap for the customer to prevent internally is not automatically a business.

Proposed 90-day targets, measured from a funded validation start:

1. Conduct 15 relevant buyer interviews. Continue only if at least three organizations describe a recurring problem with concrete examples.
2. Attempt three assisted staging installs after access and execution controls are reviewed. Record buyer-authored cases and full delivery effort.
3. Seek one signed paid scope or budget-authorized LOI. Distinguish an LOI from a purchase, invoicing from cash collection, and a pilot from a subscription.

These targets are not completed work. The $5,000 pilot and $1,000 monthly maintenance price must be tested against the actual effort and buyer budget. Scope more narrowly if customization consumes the margin.

## How the company could extend beyond services

Reuse contract patterns and adapters from permitted engagements, with private customer artifacts kept separate. Sell maintained checks for recurring releases, rather than repeatedly rebuilding a bespoke report. Measure whether later installations take less work and whether customers renew. Enterprise workflow verification and Arabic-English evaluation are later hypotheses requiring their own buyer evidence and qualified review.

Local hiring and establishment should follow supported demand and agreed program obligations. The existing plan does not justify claiming a Qatar entity, residence, institutional partner or Arabic-language capability. No outreach or customer commitments are created by this document.
