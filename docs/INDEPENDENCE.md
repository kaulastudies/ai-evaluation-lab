# Independence and Provenance Boundary

AI Evaluation Lab is an independent public engineering project.

## Allowed source classes

Public repository work should be derived only from:

1. Rama-controlled independent work and documented pre-existing know-how;
2. public/open-source references used according to their applicable licenses;
3. public research and documentation;
4. synthetic data, synthetic tasks, and synthetic examples;
5. material for which there is explicit authority to publish.

## Excluded material

Do not place into this repository:

- confidential client, contractor, employer, or partner information;
- private task-platform instructions, hidden tests, customer code, or restricted benchmark artifacts;
- customer-specific foreground deliverables;
- real regulated, patient, employee, customer, or sensitive personal data;
- credentials, production secrets, private API keys, or private infrastructure details;
- material whose source, ownership, or license is uncertain.

When provenance is uncertain, keep the material out until it is resolved.

## Public references

Public systems such as Harbor, Terminal-Bench, Inspect, METR task standards, SWE-bench, Langfuse, DeepEval, and Promptfoo may inform architecture and interoperability decisions.

Their benchmark tasks, code, documentation, trademarks, and other materials remain subject to their own licenses and restrictions. Original Lab tasks should be independently authored.

## Customer engagements

Private customer work should use an isolated customer workspace/repository and a written scope. Customer-specific deliverables do not automatically become public Lab assets.
