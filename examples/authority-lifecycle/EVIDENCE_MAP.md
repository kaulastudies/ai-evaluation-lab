## Remaining evaluation gap

These are complementary fixtures, not a composed end-to-end proof. We have **not** demonstrated revocation between an authorization service and an independently controlled receiver, with stale authority, uncertain dispatch and independently established final effects in the same experiment.

A useful next scoping step is to identify an existing external receiver interface, its final authority decision point, and its outcome-observation mechanism. The present cases can supply the controls; an adapter and agreed observation contract should precede any additional test construction.
