# Fund the AI Evaluation Lab

## Back the evidence layer for AI systems and agents

AI Evaluation Lab is an independent emerging lab building reproducible infrastructure for model and agent evaluation.

The long-term ambition is to become neutral evaluation infrastructure for an AI economy that may be measured in trillions of dollars.

That is a category ambition—not a claim of current valuation.

## What support does not buy

Support does not automatically provide:

- equity, tokens, profit share, or governance rights;
- ownership or an exclusive license to project IP;
- private repository or customer-workspace access;
- confidential datasets or benchmark answers;
- contributor or maintainer permissions;
- consulting hours;
- guaranteed prioritization;
- certification or a blanket safety guarantee.

Commercial evaluation pilots are separately scoped engagements.
