Placement Desk: record-led

A placement worker or an authenticated candidate retrieves an authorised record, checks evidence and compares explicit role requirements. This page demonstrates that proposal with fictional data.

What the published problem supports

PDS 6 in the Army Design Compendium 2025 mentions CV preparation from the existing AWPO database. It does not specify that every service, DOB, pay and qualification field is available through one identifier, or that the application is exclusively staff-facing.

Questions for the responsible team

  1. Should the primary user be a placement counsellor, the candidate, or both?
  2. Which register and identifier can be used, and who controls access?
  3. Which fields are authoritative, current and necessary? Is last pay needed at all?
  4. Who can confirm a new certificate, including one obtained after service?
  5. Which role rules, age cutoffs, relaxations and equivalences must be applied?
  6. Would the team prefer a sample export, sandbox API or supervised pilot before integration?

The prototype compares documented requirements. It does not select or reject people, submit applications or certify real credentials.
